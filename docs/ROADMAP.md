# Roadmap

This file is the plan of geo. The Status page of the site reads it, and the tracker of the project mirrors it.

| ID | Item | Status | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | The first package | done | @mark1russell7/geo 0.1.0 is on npm: points, distance, boxes, a point in a polygon, overlap, geohash and GeoJSON. |
| R11 | A limit on the longitudes of a polygon | done | A polygon with a longitude of 1e12 made `locatePoint` run for a very long time. The tests refuse a longitude out of -540 to 540. Version 0.2.0. |
| R2 | The distance from a place to an area | done | `distanceToGeometryKm` gives the great-circle distance from a point to a polygon, a line or a point. The alert system needs it to match a place and its buffer against the polygon of an alert. Version 0.2.1. |
| R3 | The alert system matches places by shape | done 2026-11-15 | The core of the alert system matches a place and its buffer against the polygon or the circle of an alert, with this package. Part 1: the region codes are the floor, and a shape adds a place in its buffer. Part 2: the shape of a CAP area block decides for that block. |
| R4 | Exact moves across the antimeridian | done 2026-11-15 | `@mark1russell7/predicates` 0.1.3 has exact predicates on moved points (algebra A27, #176), and 0.1.4 has the exact ring test `locateInRings` (A28, #178). The home of the ring test is predicates. geo 0.3.1 moves the point with no rounding, imports the ring test and drops its copy. It keeps only the rule of holes of GeoJSON and the test of a point on an open line. The limit of 1e-12 degrees goes. Version 0.3.1 (#12). |
| R14 | Fast simplification of large states | done 2026-11-15 | `simplifyRegions` of 0.3.0 takes approximately 7.4 s for the 552 NWS regions of Texas (325,410 positions), and it blocks the event loop of an alert instance. The target of alerts is under 0.5 s on a CI runner. First a bench in CI with `rigor-bench` on seeded regions with shared edges, from 10^3 to 10^6 positions, and a CPU profile. Then the changes that the profile supports, with a speed gate. The prediction comes here before the code. Part 1: the package `packages/bench` and the workflow `bench-simplify.yml` measure geo 0.3.1, its noise and its profile. In CI, `tx-552-325k` takes 5.4 s. The exact edge distance of each scanned position takes 84 % of the time. Part 2 (the fix): an exact skip bound in the scan, fewer bisection steps, a lazy tree and number ids for the topology. The prediction (made before the code): the time of `tx-552-325k` goes from 5.4 s to 150 to 500 ms, and each case keeps the output of 0.3.1. The speed gate of the bench holds the budget and the growth. The result in 5 CI runs: `tx-552-325k` takes 205 to 275 ms, and geo 0.3.1 takes 3.7 to 4.6 s. Each case keeps the output. The limits of the gate come from these runs: 360 ms and a growth of 12. Version 0.3.2 (#16). |
| R12 | Simplify regions that share edges | done | `simplifyRegions` and `regionSimplifier` simplify the counties and zones of a state for the alert map. Neighbor regions keep one shared edge, so no gap and no overlap opens. The tolerance is in km for each zoom level. Version 0.3.0. |
| R13 | Uniform values in the property tests | done | `fc.double` with a range gives values near 0 in 93 % of the draws. Each ranged property uses `uniformDouble` of `@mark1russell7/rigor-testkit`. |
| R6 | The live demo site | done 2026-11-15 | An interactive globe and flat map: pick or draw an area, move a place, and see the location, the distance, a buffer, the box and the geohash cells. The site check uses the demo at 360, 390 and 1280 pixels (#15). |
| R5 | `@procedures/geo` uses this package | done 2026-11-16 | `greatCircleKm` and the geohash of `@procedures/geo` use the functions of this package (client#642). |
| R7 | Circles as polygons | done | `circleKm` and `circleMeters` give a circle on the Earth as a GeoJSON polygon for a map, for example the circle of a CAP alert or the buffer of a place. A circle across the antimeridian is cut there. A circle around a pole has an edge along the pole. A buffer of a line or a polygon is R15. Version 0.4.0. |
| R10 | Large polygons and many places | done 2026-11-16 | `locatePoint` examines each edge of the polygon for each place, and it checks the whole polygon again at each call. The target is a polygon with 100,000 vertices and 10,000 places. Part 1: the suite `locate` of `packages/bench` measures geo 0.3.1 in CI on coasts from 10^3 to 10^6 vertices, a star and a MultiPolygon of 552 parts. Part 2: the exact test of one edge against the ray of a point goes to `@mark1russell7/predicates`, the home of the ring test. Part 3: `pointLocator` checks the polygon one time and puts its edges in bands of latitude. Each place then examines only the edges of its band. Version 0.4.1 (#19). Part 4: the speed gate of the suite `locate` holds the budget of `coast-1e5-1e4` to 86 ms and the growth to 3.1. Each limit is the worst value of 5 CI runs plus their spread. |
| R8 | The distance between two areas | done 2026-11-16 | `distanceBetweenGeometriesKm` gives the great-circle distance between the nearest points of two geometries. It is 0 when they have a common point, with the exact tests of `polygonsIntersect`. A study of 1,600 pairs of edges supports the use of the positions only, and a property test with a dense sample holds it. Version 0.4.3. |
| R9 | Geodesics on the WGS84 ellipsoid | done 2026-11-16 | `geodesicInverse` and `geodesicDirect` give the distance, the bearings and the destination on the ellipsoid, with the method of Karney. The code is a port of GeographicLib 2.2.0. Version 0.4.2 (#22). |
| R15 | Buffers of lines and polygons | done 2026-11-16 | `bufferKm` and `bufferMeters` give the area within a distance of a geometry as a GeoJSON polygon for a map. Each point within the distance less the tolerance is in it, and each point past the distance plus the tolerance is not. A property test holds the two limits. The buffer is cut at the antimeridian and has an edge along a pole. Version 0.4.4. |
| R20 | A true bound of the angle to an edge | done 2026-11-16 | The bound of the angle of a position to an edge was able to be less than the angle, very near a pole. A property test found it in CI on 2026-11-16, and main was red. Since 0.3.2, `simplifyRegions` skips a position with a bound less than the best angle, so a position near a pole was able to be lost. The fix: the bound is the larger of the box bound and the probe value, the two values of the measure. Version 0.5.3. |
| R17 | Functions for a globe | done 2026-11-16 | `toUnitVector` and `fromUnitVector` change a point to a vector on the sphere, and back. `intermediatePoint` gives the points along a great circle. `densify` adds positions to the edges of a geometry, so that a globe can draw each edge of RFC 7946 with short parts. A property test of `densify` found a rounding fault in `bboxOfGeoJson`, and this version corrects it. A command view of the alert system draws its areas on a globe with these functions. Version 0.5.0 (#26). |
| R16 | Faster buffers | done 2026-11-16 | `bufferKm` of 0.4.4 uses approximately 16 cells for each tolerance of the length of the boundary. Part 1: the suite `buffer` of `packages/bench` times `bufferKm` against geo 0.4.4 on a coast with 3 tolerances, a line near a pole and points. Its result is the class of seeded samples, so each correct buffer gives the same hash. Part 2: larger cells with the same guarantee, an early stop of the search on each edge, and the values of the block centers on the corners. The cells decrease from approximately 16 to 9 for each tolerance of length, and the trigonometric operations decrease 2.1 to 2.9 times. Version 0.5.1, with a bench run of 0.36 to 0.59 of the time of 0.4.4. The speed gate of the suite is R21. |
| R19 | The middle of a box | done 2026-11-16 | `bboxCenter` gives the middle of a bounding box, also for a box that crosses the antimeridian. A map uses it as the place of the label of a region. The closed box has the result. Version 0.5.2. |
| R18 | Speed gates that do not stop on runner noise | done 2026-11-16 | The gates `simplify` and `locate` gave no read when the two copies of head differed by more than a noise limit (12 ms and 3 ms). Under load, 4 of 36 simplify runs and 2 of 18 locate runs went over. The fix: `budgetLooksVerdict` of rigor-bench looks at 6, 12 and 24 rounds, and the bench adds rounds of the budget case when a look has no outcome. The noise limits do not change. |
| R21 | A speed gate for the suite buffer | later | No gate holds the gain of R16 at this time. The gate: a budget of the suite `buffer`, with its limit from 5 or more CI runs. It is later, because geo pauses by direction 45. |
| R22 | A bench and an index for the distance between two areas | later | `distanceBetweenGeometriesKm` of R8 examines each pair of a position and an edge, so its time grows as n·m. Part 1: the suite `distance` of `packages/bench` times two polygons of 10^2, 10^3 and 10^4 vertices in CI. Part 2: when the 10^4 case takes more than 100 ms, an index of the edges examines the nearest pairs first. Part 3: a speed gate holds the gain, with its limit from 5 or more CI runs. It is later, because geo pauses by direction 45. |
