Points on the Earth, as plain functions

geo gives the distance, the bearing, the area that holds a place, the box and the geohash. It has no dependencies on a map library. Move the place, turn the globe, or draw an area.

pnpm add @mark1russell7/geo

Tap the map to add the corners of the area.

  • The area
  • The buffer of the place
  • The geohash cell and its neighbors
  • The bounding box of the area
